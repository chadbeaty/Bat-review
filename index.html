Why almonds?

Almonds give a hot sauce a rounder body. They soften sharp heat, carry flavor, and add plant protein to whatever you pour it on.

Not for you? Choose No Almonds in step 3 and we'll leave them out.

Questions

Do I have to get almonds?

No. Choose No Almonds in step 3 and your sauce is made without them.

Is your kitchen nut-free?

No. Every sauce, including those without almonds, is made in a kitchen that handles tree nuts. If you have a tree nut allergy, our sauces are not safe for you.

How long until my order ships?

Every bottle is made to order. Most orders ship within 5 business days.

Can I order more than one recipe?

Yes. Build a bottle, add it to your order, then build another. Each one can have its own name.

How should I store it?

Refrigerate after opening and shake before use. Sauces with almonds can settle a little, which is normal.
