Built for one game.

General swing apps are built for every hitter, so slow pitch gets treated like baseball with a bigger ball. Gapper is the performance analytics platform for the slow-pitch softball community: the 12-inch ball, the arc and the four-outfielder defense, measured on their own terms. Built for slowpitch. Nothing else.

  1. 6–12ft

    The pitch drops in.

    A high arc, not a fastball. Your timing is about the drop, so Gapper times your load and stride against it.

  2. 0.2s

    Land the front heel, then swing.

    Skilled slow-pitch hitters plant the front heel about 0.2 seconds before contact. Gapper grades your front-foot landing on that slow-pitch window, not baseball’s “get your foot down early.”

  3. 3gaps

    Four outfielders. Three gaps.

    Ten fielders, four of them in the outfield. Line drives into the gaps win games, so Gapper tracks your line-drive rate and where you hit it: pull side, middle, opposite field.

Record. Analyze. Improve.

Record

Prop your phone on a fence or tripod, 10 to 15 feet away, waist high, whole body in the picture. Shoes included.

  • Hit live. Gapper catches every swing by itself and gives you feedback before the next pitch.
  • Upload a video. Any clip from your camera roll. Every frame gets analyzed.
  • Film in slo-mo. At 120 to 240 fps Gapper also measures exit velo, launch angle and pitch speed.

Analyze

Gapper reads the swing from the ground up: when the front foot lands, how the front leg firms up, how the hips and shoulders turn, where the hands go.

Improve

One fix, not ten tips. Then the drill for it, a 10-swing challenge, and your Hitting Profile to show it’s working.

The one fix for that swing

Your hands dropped during the load. Keep them at shoulder height as you start the swing.

Drill: Hands-at-the-shoulder holds. Take your stance, load, and freeze for two seconds at launch with your hands still at shoulder height. Ten reps, then swing for real.

What Gapper measures, and how sure it is.
