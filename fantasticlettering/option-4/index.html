Questions

Good to know before you order.

Don't see your question? Call (331) 305-2468. A real person picks up during business hours.

What's the minimum order?

Screen printing starts at 24 pieces per design. Embroidery starts at 12 pieces. Smaller runs are sometimes possible, so just ask.

How long does an order take?

Most orders ship within about 10 business days after you approve your mockup. Rush orders are often possible. Tell us your in-hands date and we'll be straight with you.

What file should I send for my logo?

Vector files (AI, EPS, PDF or SVG) work best. A high-resolution PNG or JPG also works. If all you have is a blurry logo, our art team can clean it up or redraw it.

Can I bring my own shirts?

In many cases, yes. Contact us first so we can check the fabric and quantity. We usually save you money by supplying the garments at wholesale prices.

Do you offer mixed sizes and colors?

Yes. Mix sizes freely within an order. Different garment colors are fine too, and the pricing barely changes.

Do you set up accounts for businesses and schools?

Yes. Repeat customers get their artwork kept on file, simple reorders, and invoicing options for schools and businesses. See uniform programs.

Will I see a proof before you print?

Always. You'll get a digital mockup showing the placement, size and colors. Nothing goes to press until you approve it.

Can you rush an order?

Often, yes. Rush orders usually ship in about 5 business days after you approve your mockup, for an added fee. Tell us your in-hands date and we'll confirm right away.

Do you need a deposit?

Payment terms are confirmed with your quote. First orders usually need a deposit, and established business and school accounts can be invoiced.

Can you deliver or ship?

Pick up free at our Jefferson Park shop, or we can deliver locally or ship anywhere in the U.S. Delivery options and costs are listed on your quote.

How do reorders work?

Call or email with what you need and how many. Your artwork, ink colors and garment choices are already on file, so there's no new design work and no new embroidery setup fee.
