Every print starts with a screen.

One screen for every ink color, one pass of the squeegee for every screen. Here is a single shirt, from blank to finished.

One shirt, start to finish.

A blank charcoal tee on the press.

  1. Art

    Your logo is cleaned up and separated into one layer for each ink color.

  2. Screen

    Each layer is exposed onto its own mesh screen. Open mesh is where ink will pass.

  3. Ink

    Ink is matched to your colors and laid along the top of the screen.

  4. Pull

    The squeegee pushes ink through the mesh, one color and one screen at a time.

    White baseOrangeBlack
  5. Cure

    Heat sets the ink into the fabric so the print lasts wash after wash.
