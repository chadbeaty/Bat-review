# BRIEF: Fantastic Lettering, "Press run" (option 6)

Interviewed 2026-11-18 (multiple-choice answers picked by the owner's agent, Chad).
Concept site to pitch Fantastic Lettering, a commercial and retail screen printing and
embroidery shop at 5644 W Lawrence Ave, Chicago, since 1984.

## The eight topics (answers as given)

1. **Vibe:** "Screen printing shop" (typed answer).
2. **Journey, in order:** "Craft first": see a print being made, then what the shop does,
   then who they print for, then uniform accounts, then get a quote.
3. **Energy curve:** "Calm, build, big middle": quiet open, energy builds, the
   shirt-printing moment hits hard in the middle, calm confident close.
4. **The one moment:** "A shirt prints as I scroll": each scroll pulls the squeegee and
   lays down one ink color at a time until the finished design appears.
5. **What no other site does:** "Watch it in real steps": each ink pass is labeled with the
   real shop step: art, screen, ink, pull, cure.
6. **Range:** "Premium-minimal".
7. **Structure:** "Separate scenes".
8. **Assets:** "Reuse + code animation": reuse the 10 placeholder product photos from
   option 5, draw the printing animation in code. No new generation, no spend.

## Journey (beats)

1. Arrival: the shop's name, nothing else, finding register.
2. Quiet: every print starts with a screen.
3. The run: one shirt printed start to finish, step by step. (Peak)
4. Range: what leaves the shop.
5. Recognition: who it is for.
6. Trust: uniform programs for repeat accounts.
7. Commitment: start a print run (one action, one label everywhere: "Start a print run").

## Feeling curve (written before the acts)

| Act | Feeling | What causes it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Title page | Calm, curiosity | The name set huge in three ink layers that drift into perfect register as you scroll. Nothing else moves. |
| 2 Screen | Intimacy, quiet | One macro photograph of a squeegee on a screen, wiped into view, two plain sentences. Authored quiet before the peak. |
| 3 The run | Awe, delight | A blank charcoal shirt on the stage; scrolling draws the art, lowers the screen, lays the ink, pulls three colors one pass at a time, then cures it. A step ledger names each real step as it happens. |
| 4 What leaves the shop | Appetite | Garments travel sideways, each one labeled like an object, not pitched. |
| 5 Who it is for | Recognition | Plain large type naming the kinds of customers, so a visitor finds themselves. |
| 6 Uniforms | Confidence | One photograph of folded uniforms beside four short facts about repeat accounts. |
| 7 Start a print run | Resolve | The page goes quiet and becomes a sentence you fill in. |

## The peak

> "I scrolled and watched a shirt actually get printed, one color at a time, and it told me which step I was looking at."

Lives in act 3. It gets the largest span on the page, the silence of act 2 before it,
and the only bespoke animation on the page.

## Tell-someone sentence

It's the site where scrolling prints a shirt in front of you, pass by pass, and names
every step the shop really does.

## Authored silence

Act 2 is deliberately quiet (one image, two sentences) so the run has something to arrive
from. It is not dead scroll.

## Honesty constraints

- No invented numbers, testimonials, history, or turnaround claims. No counters.
- Photos are AI-generated placeholders with made-up sample emblems; say so on the page.
- The quote form is not connected; say so when submitted.
