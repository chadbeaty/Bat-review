# Scoring

Score each category 0-100, then combine with these weights. The owner can change the weights.

| Category | Weight | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Early momentum | 25 | Is it just starting to get attention? |
| Demand acceleration | 20 | Are mentions, views and searches speeding up? |
| Competition | 20 | Are relatively few sellers promoting it? (higher = less crowded) |
| Organic video potential | 15 | Can it be shown compellingly in 10-30 seconds? |
| Problem-solving appeal | 10 | Does it solve an obvious problem, or create a strong wow? |
| Profit potential | 10 | Can it sell profitably after all costs? |

**Missing data:** if you couldn't assess a category, write "unknown" and count it as **25** in the total. This
keeps a thinly researched product from scoring high.

## How to score each category

**Early momentum**
- How new: first seen 0-3 days ago = 1.0; up to 7 days = 0.85; up to 14 = 0.65; up to 30 = 0.4; up to 60 = 0.2; older = 0.1
- Activity = the smaller of 1 and (mentions in the last 72 h × 0.4 + different creators this week × 0.6) ÷ 3
- Score = 100 × how new × activity. One creator, one mention ≈ 33. Two creators ≈ 67. Three or more ≈ 100 (if fresh).

**Demand acceleration**
- Compare mentions in the last 3 days with the pace before that (use history). Same pace ≈ 0, double ≈ 33, 4× ≈ 67, 8× or more = 100.
- With only one sighting ever, cap at 40 (one data point can't show acceleration).
- If views on the same videos grew between runs, or search results show a fresh burst of coverage, average that signal in.

**Competition (higher = less crowded)**
- Count the different creators and sellers seen promoting it (tracked creators plus search results).
- Raw score = 100 − 12 × that count (minimum 0).
- The evidence only covers part of the market, so pull toward a cautious 40: score = 40 + 0.3 × (raw − 40). With little data, competition should land between about 40 and 58, never high.
- If it is clearly everywhere (many stores, Amazon best-seller mentions, lots of TikTok Shop listings in results), score it low and flag it as saturated.

**Organic video potential** (judgement): a visual transformation or satisfying action, instantly clear, easy to film = high. Needs explaining, has an invisible benefit, or is hard to film = low. Give the reason.

**Problem-solving appeal** (judgement): an everyday, widely felt problem, or a strong wow reaction = high. Give the reason.

**Profit potential** (estimates, label them):
- Estimate the supplier cost, shipping (default $4) and a realistic selling price from comparable generic goods.
- Margin = price − cost − shipping − price × 11% (payment fees 2.9%, refunds 5%, other 3%) − $0.30.
- Raw score = 100 × (margin% − 10%) ÷ 50%, between 0 and 100. If the margin is under $8 per order, multiply by margin ÷ 8.
- Estimates aren't quotes, so pull toward 40: score = 40 + 0.6 × (raw − 40).

## Confidence

- **High**: all six categories assessed, 3+ different creators, seen on 2+ platforms, and seller presence checked.
- **Medium**: at least five categories assessed and 2+ different creators.
- **Low**: anything less. Say what's missing.

## Saturated

Call a product saturated when 8 or more different creators promoted it in one week, or search results show it
widely sold (many stores, Amazon best-seller lists). If it was previously promising (60+), flag it in "Avoid".
